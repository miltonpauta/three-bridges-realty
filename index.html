How we can help

Whether it's your first apartment or your third building, we handle the details and explain every step.

Buying your first home

We guide you through mortgage pre-approval and down payment help like SONYMA and FHA loans, and explain every document.

Selling your home

A price based on real sales on your block, professional photos, and a listing shared with buyers in three languages.

Renting

Apartments for tenants and qualified tenants for landlords, with leases, deposits and fees explained clearly.

Multi-family and investment

Two- and three-family homes where rent from the other units helps pay the mortgage.

Co-op board packages

Evening and weekend showings

How it works

Buying a home takes about 60 to 90 days in New York. Here's what that looks like with us.

  1. Talk with us

    A free, no-pressure conversation about your budget, family and neighborhood.

  2. Get your finances ready

    We connect you with trusted lenders and assistance programs.

  3. Find the right home

    We tour homes with you and negotiate the best price.

  4. Close with confidence

    We stay with you through inspection, contract and closing day.
